Common questions

Answered before you ask.

Do I need a LearnWorlds account before we start?

No — you do not need an existing LearnWorlds account. During the strategy call, I will recommend the right plan tier for your needs and scale. If you already have an account and an existing academy, that is ideal for the Growth Engine programme — I begin with a full audit of what you have built.

Do I need to have my course content recorded before we start?

Not necessarily. The Academy Foundation programme includes full curriculum design — which means I plan the module structure, lesson content requirements, and video briefs before you record anything. You will know exactly what to film, how long each lesson should be, and what format to use. Having some content ready speeds things up, but it is not a prerequisite for starting.

How long does a full academy build take?

Academy Foundation typically takes 8–10 weeks depending on course complexity, number of modules, and how quickly content is provided for upload. A focused 8-pillar course can be live in 2–3 weeks. A multi-path academy with community features and a full launch funnel takes 8–10 weeks. Growth Engine audit and optimisation work typically produces measurable results within 30 days of implementation.

Which programme is right for me — Foundation or Growth Engine?

If you have no live academy yet — or have started building one but not launched — Academy Foundation is your programme. If you have a live LearnWorlds academy that is either under-enrolling, has low completion rates, or is not generating the revenue it should, Growth Engine is the correct fit. If you are genuinely unsure, book the strategy call and I will diagnose your situation and tell you directly which programme applies.

Can you also help with marketing and selling the course — not just the technical build?

Yes — both programmes include a launch strategy component: sales page copywriting, email launch sequence, and social media content for the launch period. For clients who want a full end-to-end marketing system — lead magnets, funnels, paid advertising, and ongoing social media management — I offer these as additional services that run alongside or after the academy build.

Will I be able to manage the academy myself after it is built?

Yes — every project includes a structured handover session and written documentation so you can add new lessons, update content, and manage your student community independently. LearnWorlds is designed to be operated confidently by non-technical users once it is properly set up. You own the platform, the content, and all student data outright — no ongoing dependency on me to keep it running.
