Common questions

Answered before you ask.

What if I already have content — can you just design it?

Absolutely. If you have existing content — whether that is a rough draft, blog posts, course transcripts, or speaker notes — I can take that raw material, restructure it into a coherent eBook, clean up the copy, and produce the full design. Design-only projects are also available if your copy is already finalised and polished.

How long does an eBook project take?

Most projects are completed in 5–10 business days depending on length and complexity. A short lead magnet (10–20 pages) typically takes 5–7 days. A longer framework eBook or whitepaper (40–60 pages) takes 8–12 days. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee — just ask and I will confirm availability.

Will the eBook sound like me, or like AI?

It will sound like you — that is a non-negotiable part of how I work. Every eBook project starts with a voice and tone discovery process where I study how you write and communicate. AI tools are used for research, structure, and efficiency — but all final copy is written, edited, and reviewed by a human to ensure it sounds authentic and specific to you.

How many revisions are included?

Revisions are included until you are satisfied — there is no hard limit. In practice, most projects require one or two rounds of minor feedback after the draft is delivered. Structural changes after outline approval or significant scope changes may be quoted separately, but I will always communicate that clearly before proceeding.

Do I get the editable source file?

Yes — the editable source file (Canva or equivalent) is included in every project. This means you can make minor updates, change a page, or add a chapter in the future without needing to come back for every small change. The final PDF in both print-ready and web-optimised versions is also included as standard.

Can you also create the opt-in landing page to distribute it?

Yes — landing page copywriting and build is available as an add-on to any eBook project. I can write the opt-in page headline, body copy, and button text, and build the actual page if needed. Email automation setup (connecting the form to a welcome sequence) is also available. Ask for a bundled quote when you reach out.
