Common questions

Answered before you ask.

Do I need to appear on camera?

No — camera-shy clients are very welcome. Several of the video formats I produce require zero on-camera presence: AI avatar video, voiceover-led slideshow video, screen recordings, text-motion graphics, and repurposed content from podcasts or webinars. I will discuss which formats make the most sense for your business and your comfort level during the strategy session.

What if I already have existing footage I have never edited?

Existing footage is ideal — it means I can start producing content immediately without any new filming. Send me whatever you have: phone recordings, webinar recordings, Zoom calls, podcast episodes, conference presentations, or old YouTube videos. I will identify the best clips, edit them into short-form content, and optimise each one for the platform it will live on.

How many videos do I get per month?

This depends on your package. A standard monthly video retainer covers 8–12 short-form videos across your active platforms, including scripting, editing, captions, and platform optimisation. Projects are also available if you want a specific batch of videos without a monthly commitment — for example, 6 videos for a launch campaign or 10 clips from a single recording.

What format do I need to send my raw footage in?

Any standard format works: MP4, MOV, or AVI. Phone-recorded footage is perfectly fine — most modern phone cameras produce high enough quality for social media video. If you are filming yourself, I will provide a simple filming brief that takes most clients under 10 minutes per video. No professional equipment required.

Will the videos actually sound like me?

Yes — voice and tone matching is a core part of the scripting process. All scripts are written after a voice discovery session where I study how you naturally speak and communicate. AI tools are used for speed and structure, but all final scripts are written and reviewed by a human to ensure they sound like you — not like a generic AI output.

Can you handle posting and scheduling as well as editing?

Posting and scheduling support is included in monthly retainers. I prepare the caption, select the hashtags, recommend the posting time, and either post directly or prepare everything in a scheduling tool for your approval. You are involved for any final approval — nothing goes live without your sign-off unless you specifically request fully delegated posting.
