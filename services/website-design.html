Projects

Your website should be your best salesperson. Right now it probably isn't.

A website that does not convert is not just a missed opportunity — it is actively working against you. Every visitor who leaves without taking action is a potential client you will never hear from again.

❌

It looks outdated or unpolished

In the first 3 seconds, visitors judge whether you are the right choice. An outdated site loses that judgment before a single word is read.

❌

It does not clearly explain what you do

If a visitor has to work out what you offer and who it is for, they will leave. Clarity converts — confusion sends people to your competitors.

❌

No clear next step for visitors

Without a compelling CTA in the right place, visitors scroll to the bottom and close the tab. Traffic without conversion is just a vanity metric.

❌

Not optimised for mobile or search

Over 60% of web traffic is mobile. If your site breaks on a phone or does not appear in search, you are invisible to the majority of your potential clients.

Common questions

Answered before you ask.

Which platform will you build my site on?

That depends on your needs, technical comfort, and budget. I build on WordPress (most flexible, best for long-term SEO), Webflow (premium design control), GoHighLevel (built-in CRM and funnels), LearnWorlds (academy and course pages), Carrd (clean single-page sites), and Google Sites (simple, low-maintenance). During the project brief, I will recommend the platform that best fits your situation — not the one that is easiest for me to build on.

Do you write the copy or do I need to provide it?

Full copywriting is included in every project. I write the headlines, body copy, CTAs, and meta descriptions — all in your voice, using conversion copywriting frameworks. If you have existing copy you want to keep, I can review and improve it as part of the process. You will never receive a design mockup with placeholder text asking you to “fill in the content later.”

How long does a website project take?

A focused landing page takes 5–7 business days. A full multi-page website typically takes 3–4 weeks. Timeline depends on the number of pages, platform complexity, and how quickly feedback is provided at each review stage. Rush delivery is available for time-sensitive projects — ask and I will confirm availability.

Will I be able to update the site myself after it is built?

Yes — that is a non-negotiable part of the handover. Every project includes a walkthrough session and written documentation so you can update text, images, and content without needing to come back for every small change. You will own the site outright — no ongoing dependency on me to keep it running.

Do you handle hosting and domain setup?

I can advise on and set up hosting and domain configuration as part of the project. You own and pay for your own hosting and domain — I connect everything and handle the technical setup. I recommend keeping hosting in your own name so you are never dependent on a third party to access your own website.

Can you redesign an existing site rather than build from scratch?

Absolutely — redesign and optimisation projects are available. I will audit your existing site, identify the specific conversion and design gaps, and rebuild or improve the sections that matter most. In some cases a partial redesign (hero, CTA strategy, service pages) produces better results faster than a full rebuild.
