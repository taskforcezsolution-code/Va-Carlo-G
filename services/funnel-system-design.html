Common questions

Answered before you ask.

What is a funnel and do I actually need one?

A funnel is a connected sequence of steps designed to move a stranger toward becoming a client — automatically. If you have traffic or followers but are not consistently converting them into leads and clients without manual effort, you need a funnel. Without one, every potential client either gets lost or requires you to be personally active to convert them. That is not scalable.

Which platform will you build the funnel on?

I build on the platform that best fits your needs and existing tools. GoHighLevel is my primary recommendation for businesses that want everything — CRM, email, pages, and automation — in one place. For businesses already using WordPress, Webflow, or Mailchimp, I build within those. I will never recommend switching platforms unless there is a strong reason to do so.

Does the funnel include copywriting or do I need to provide it?

Full copywriting is included in every funnel project — landing pages, email sequences, sales pages, and CTAs. All copy is written in your voice using conversion frameworks (PAS, AIDA, StoryBrand). You will never receive a template with placeholder text asking you to fill in the gaps.

How long does a funnel build take?

A lead generation funnel (landing page + email welcome sequence) takes 7–10 business days. A full evergreen sales funnel with multiple email sequences, sales page, and checkout typically takes 3–4 weeks. Launch funnels with pre-launch sequences are scoped individually. Timeline depends on complexity and how quickly feedback is provided at each review stage.

Will I be able to make changes to the funnel myself after it is built?

Yes — every funnel handover includes a walkthrough session and written documentation. You will be able to update email content, change landing page copy, and adjust automations without needing to come back for every small change. You own the funnel outright.

Can you audit and improve my existing funnel instead of building from scratch?

Absolutely — funnel audits and optimisation projects are available separately. I will review every stage of your current funnel, identify where leads are dropping off, and produce a prioritised list of improvements. In many cases a targeted optimisation (fixing the landing page conversion rate or rewriting the email sequence) produces better results faster than a full rebuild.
