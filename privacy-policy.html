Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VA Carlo G. ("I", "me", "my") handles information on vacarlog.taskforcezsolution.com (the "Website"). I keep data collection to a minimum.

1. Information I collect

  • Information you send me. When you use a contact, quote, or audit request form, the details you enter (such as your name, email address, and message) are submitted through Tally and stored in my Tally account so I can reply. If you book a call, the information you enter is handled by Calendly.
  • Cookie and browser storage. The Website stores your cookie choice (a cookie named user_consent, kept for up to 5 years) and your light/dark theme preference in your browser. Nothing else is stored by the Website itself.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, the hosting provider and third-party services described below may automatically receive your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

2. How I use information

  • To reply to your enquiries and schedule calls.
  • To remember your cookie and display preferences.
  • To keep the Website working and secure.

I do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies

The Website uses only a small amount of browser storage, as described above. If you choose Reject All, your choice is saved so the banner does not appear again. You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. If analytics or advertising tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

4. Third-party services

The Website relies on outside services that may receive technical data such as your IP address when you load or use them. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr (fonts and code libraries)
  • Wistia (welcome video)
  • Calendly (booking calls)
  • Tawk.to (live chat and chatbot: messages you type in the chat, and basic details such as your IP address and browser, are processed by Tawk.to and may use cookies)
  • Tally (contact and request forms)
  • LinkedIn and Facebook (only if you click my social links)

5. Data retention

I keep emails and enquiry details only as long as needed to respond to you, deliver agreed services, and meet any legal or accounting obligations. You can ask me to delete your information at any time.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the United States, Australia, the European Economic Area or the Philippines), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. To make a request, email me at the address below.

7. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or email transmission is completely secure. Please do not send passwords or sensitive financial details through any form.

8. Children

The Website is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 16. I do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: butch@taskforcezsolution.com

This is a general-purpose policy written for a simple portfolio website. It is not legal advice. If you start collecting more data (analytics, ads, newsletters, payments), have it reviewed and updated.
